The first draft of White Paper WP-01 described its results as empirical. Nothing had been measured. Each checkable number was recomputed by the code in the repository from the paper's own equations and parameters, and the revised paper and the book use the model's values. All values are calculated or simulated.

A27Tether: chance one 5 ms scan hears the child's bandWP-01 section V-Cassumed 10.48 %design fix

A 5 ms window every 5 s almost never coincides with an advertisement sent every 800 ms: four scans in a row all miss 98% of the time with the child beside the parent. A window of 811 ms cannot miss; it costs 746 uA.

A28Tether: separation at which the alert soundsWP-01 section V-C25-30 m174 m design fix

Losing the signal happens at the edge of radio range, not at 25-30 m. To alert at 30 m the parent tag must treat an RSSI below about -78 dBm as a miss.

A34Supply during the pulse with the 100 uF capacitorWP-01 section VI-C2.681.61 Vdesign fix

100 uF behind 42 ohm has a time constant of 4.2 ms, far shorter than the 100 ms pulse, so it does not hold the supply up. Either the capacitor must be at least 895 uF or the pulse current at most 29.8 mA.

A37Pseudonyms are unique in the lookup tableWP-01 section IV-Dassumed10% chance of a clash design fix

With 10,000 tags and a three-slot window, two entries share a 32-bit pseudonym about one time in ten. The table therefore maps a pseudonym to a list and separates clashes by the slot counter.

A38Replayed frames are rejectedWP-01 section IV-Aalloutside the 3-slot window design fix

Arrival tuples carry no MIC and poles hold no keys, so a frame replayed at another pole within its own slot window (up to 45 minutes) is accepted as a sighting. Only SOS frames are fully verified.

A39Bloom heartbeat false-positive rateWP-01 section V-Bnot stated1.6% at 50 tags, 57% at 300 open

64 bytes hold about 78 wearables at a 5% error rate. Dense hub poles are on fibre and can send a larger filter.

A40Pole spacing on the LoRa tracksWP-01 section III-A25-100 m108 m differs

195 poles over 21.0 km of track is one pole per 108 m. Still inside the Coded PHY range of Eq. 2.

A41Wearable transmit power on the nRF52811WP-01 section III-B+8 dBm+4 dBm open

The nRF52811 is specified up to +4 dBm. +8 dBm needs a part such as the nRF52820 or nRF52833. To be confirmed against the datasheet before the bill of materials is fixed.

A42SOS reaches the control room within 4 s (3,000-tag surge)WP-01 section Abstractall92% (median 0.5 s) differs

Simulation. The first LoRa attempt is lost 23% of the time under the surge, and a repeat waits an assumed 3 s. On fibre poles every SOS arrives in under a second.
