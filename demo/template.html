The signed frame

Fifteen bytes, built with real AES-CMAC. Change the slot and the identifier, the address and the signature all change. Press SOS and only two bytes of content differ, but the signature is new.

A gateway cell in a surge

Every report costs LoRa airtime on eight shared channels. Crowd the cell and see which design keeps its frames.

The 64-byte heartbeat

A pole says who is present with 512 bits and three probes per wristband. It never misses someone who is there. As it fills, it starts to say yes for people who are not.

The server only asks a heartbeat about wristbands it already places at that pole, so false positives stay rare in use. Hub poles are on fibre and can send a larger filter.

Battery, and the cold buzzer

Coded PHY buys range and pays in airtime. And at −5 °C a coin cell's internal resistance turns a 32 mA buzzer pulse into a brown-out unless the capacitor is large enough.
